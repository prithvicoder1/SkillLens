## What is SkillLens?

Most job seekers apply blindly — submitting the same resume to 100 companies without knowing exactly what skills they are missing for each role. SkillLens solves this.

You upload your resume. SkillLens reads it using NLP, extracts your skills and experience, then semantically retrieves the most relevant job descriptions from a vector database of thousands of real postings. A fine-tuned RAG pipeline powered by an LLM then generates a precise, personalized skill-gap report — ranked by priority, with time estimates. It also simulates a full mock interview for your target role, evaluates your answers in real time, and gives you a hiring probability score with explainability.

This is not a chatbot. This is a career intelligence system.
